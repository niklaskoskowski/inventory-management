A cycle is one loop turn: html5-qrcode decodes, and on failure decodes the mirrored canvas too, then sleeps 1000/fps = 100 ms before the next turn. "decode calls/s" counts every zxing call, "frame cycles/s" counts loop turns (a call more than 50 ms after the previous one starts a new turn), and "ms per zxing call" is the gap inside a pair — the mirrored decode, i.e. the cost of one zxing pass on this phone with no sleep in it. ms/cycle minus that minus 100 ms is roughly the first decode plus the drawImage. Flip retry OFF is not what the app does today — it is here to price the second decode.
